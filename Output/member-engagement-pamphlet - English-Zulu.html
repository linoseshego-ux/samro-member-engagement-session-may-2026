ISIKHWAMA SOMHLALAPHANSI SAKWA-SAMRO

Izincazelo Ezibalulekile

Imali eseSikhwameni — Isamba semali okhokhelwa yona kanye nenzuzo yezimali ezitshaliwe noma nini ngesikhathi sobulungu bakho. Ikhonjisiwe esitatimendeni sakho senzuzo zonyaka.

Isikhwama esinemingcele ecacile — Inzuzo yakho yomhlalaphansi ixhomeke emalini u-SAMRO ayikhokhele eSikhwameni kanye nokuthi izimali ezitshaliwe zenzé kahle kangakanani.

Izindlalifa nabantu abangaphansi kwakho — Labo owabakhethela kanye nezindlalifa zakho ngomthetho (izingane, umlingani, abazali, njll.) abamukela inzuzo yakho yokushona.

Imininingwane Ebalulekile

  • Ungathatha umhlalaphansi kuphela uma uneminyaka engama-55 noma ngaphezulu.
  • Ukuthatha ngaphambi kuka-55 ngenxa yesifo kudinga ubufakazi obugcwele kanye nemvume yaBaPhathi.
  • Uma inzuzo yakho yomhlalaphansi isikhokhiwe, ngeke uvunyelwe ukubuya eSikhwameni.
  • Zonke izinzuzo zingahlawulwa intela ngosuku lokukhokhwa ngokwemithetho yentela.

Ukuphathwa kweSikhwama

ISikhwama siphethwe yiBhodi laBasingethe iSikhwama abayisi-9: aba-3 abaqokwe nguMsunguli (i-SAMRO), abayisi-5 abaqokwe amalungu, no-1 ozimele. IBhodi lihlangana njalo ezinyangeni ezintathu; umhlangano waminyaka yonke uyabanjwa.

KEY DEFINITIONS & NOTES

Fund Credit

The total of contributions paid on your behalf plus investment returns, at any point during your membership. Shown on your annual benefit statement.

Defined Contribution Fund

Your retirement benefit depends on the contributions paid and how well the Fund's investments have performed.

Beneficiaries & Dependants

Beneficiaries are those you nominate, plus your legal dependants (children, spouse, parents, grandchildren, etc.), who receive your death benefit.

Important Notes

  • You may not retire before age 55.
  • Retirement before age 55 on ill-health grounds requires conclusive medical evidence and Trustee approval.
  • Once your retirement benefit is settled you may not re-join the Fund.
  • All benefits may be subject to income tax on the date of payment.
  • The Fund Rules govern in all cases and are available on request.

Fund Governance

A Board of 9 Trustees — 3 Founder-appointed (SAMRO), 5 member-elected, 1 independent — oversees the Fund. The Board meets quarterly; an AGM is held annually where members receive a full report back.

SAMRO RETIREMENT ANNUITY FUND

Membership

Open to all SAMRO members under age 70 who earn, or have earned, at least R100 in royalties per year. The Fund has operated since 1 January 1969.

How the Fund Works

SAMRO pays a contribution on your behalf each year. Investment managers invest these funds so they grow over time. Your fund credit grows with contributions and investment returns — which may be positive or negative depending on market conditions.

Contributions

SAMRO contributes annually — the greater of:

  • R800; or
  • The royalty-linked portion allocated by the Founder on behalf of members.

Fund expenses (auditors, actuary) are deducted before the balance is invested.

Retirement Benefits

At retirement, your benefit equals your fund credit. You may:

  • Take up to one-third (1/3) as a cash lump sum; and
  • Use the remaining two-thirds (2/3) to purchase an annuity (pension) from a registered life assurer.

The full amount may only be commuted to a lump sum if your total benefit is below the SARS threshold at retirement. Seek independent financial advice before selecting an annuity. The Fund holds a with-profit annuity arrangement — contact us for details.

Death Benefits

If you die before retirement, a lump sum equal to your full fund credit is payable to your dependants and nominees. The Trustees distribute the benefit in terms of Section 37C of the Pension Funds Act and are not bound solely by your nomination.

Funeral Benefit

SAMRO operates a funeral benefit scheme to assist you and your immediate family with funeral expenses. Contact SAMRO for details.

LETLOLE LA SAMRO LA HO BEHA MEJA FATSHE

Botho

Letlole le buletswe ditho tsohle tsa SAMRO tse sa fihlellang dilemo tse 70, tse amohelang bonyane R100 ka selemo. Letlole le thehilwe ka la 1 Pherekgong 1969.

Letlole le Sebetsa Jwang

SAMRO e lefa tjhelete Letloleng bakeng sa hao selemo le selemo. Batsamaisi ba matsete ba tsetela tjhelete hore e hole. Mokitlane wa hao o hola ka dinyehelo le ditefello tsa matsete — tse ka bang tse ntle kapa tse mpe ho itshetlehile maemong a dimmaraka.

Dinyehelo

SAMRO e nyehela selemo le selemo — tjhelete e fetang e latelang:

  • R800; kapa
  • Karolo ya ditefello tse fumanweng e abetsweng ke Mothehi bakeng sa ditho.

Ditjeo tsa Letlole (balebedi ba dibuka, aktiyueri) di hulwa pele ho tsetelo ya e setseng.

Dikuno tsa ho Beha Meja Fatshe

Ha o beha meja fatshe, kuno ya hao e lekana le Mokitlane wa Letlole. O ka nka:

  • Ho fihlela ho karolo e le nngwe borarong (1/3) e le kontane e kgolo; le
  • Pedi borarong (2/3) ho reka annuity ho tswa ho mofani wa inshorense e ngodisitsweng.

Tjhelete e felletseng e ka fetolwa kontane feela haeba kuno ya hao e le ka tlase ho tekanyo ya SARS. Batla keletso ya ditjhelete ya profeshenale pele o kgetha mofuta wa annuity. Letlole le na le pelaelo ya annuity e nang le phaello — ikopanye le rona bakeng sa dintlha.

Dikuno tsa Lefu

Ha o hlokahala pele ho ho beha meja fatshe, kontane e kgolo e lekanang le Mokitlane wa Letlole wa hao e tla arolelwa baamohedi le ba kgethilweng ho latela Karolo 37C ya Molao wa Letlole la Penshene. Bahlokomedi ba nka qeto ya phetoho ya karolo mme ha ba tlamehelwe ke kgetho ya hao feela.

Dikuno tsa Lepato

SAMRO e tsamaisa sekema sa dikuno tsa lepato ho thusa wena le ba lelapa la hao ka ditjeo tsa lepato. Ikopanye le SAMRO bakeng sa dintlha.

ISIKHWAMA SOMHLALAPHANSI SAKWA-SAMRO

Ubulungu

Ubulungu buvuleleke kuwo onke amalungu e-SAMRO angakabi neminyaka engama-70, athola noma ake athola imali engekho ngaphansi kuka-R100 ngonyaka. ISikhwama sasungulwa mhla lu-1 kuMasinagana kowe-1969.

Ngabe iSikhwama Sisebenza Kanjani

I-SAMRO ikukhokhela eSikhwameni njalo ngonyaka. Abaphathi bezimali batshalwa lezi zimali ukuze zikhule. Imali yakho eseSikhwameni ikhula ngezimali ezikhokhiwe nezinzuzo — ezingaba nzuri noma zimbi ngokwezimo zamazwe omnotho.

Izimali Ezikhokhwa eSikhwameni

I-SAMRO ikhokhela njalo ngonyaka — inani elikhulu kuno:

  • R800; noma
  • Ingxenye yemali ezuzwa amalungu eyabelwe uMsunguli egameni lamalungu.

Izindleko zeSikhwama (abacwaningimabhuku, ongoti wezezimali) zibanjwa ngaphambi kokutshala imali esele.

Izinzuzo Zomhlalaphansi

Uma usuthatha umhlalaphansi, inzuzo yakho ilingana nemali yakho eseSikhwameni. Ungathatha:

  • Kuphela ingxenye eyodwa yokuthathu (1/3) njengemali eyisamba; futhi
  • U-2/3 osalayo uthenge ngayo impesheni enkampanini yomshwalense wempilo ebhalisile.

Isamba esigcwele singaguqulelwa kuphela uma inzuzo yakho ingaphansi komkhawulo we-SARS. Dinga iseluleko sezezimali ezizimele ngaphambi kokukhetha impesheni. ISikhwama sinesu lempesheni enenzuzo — xhumana nathi ukuze uthole imininingwane.

Izinzuzo Zokushona

Uma kwenzeka ushona ngaphambi komhlalaphansi, isamba esilingana nemali yakho eseSikhwameni siyokhokhwa izindlalifa nezilungu ezikhethiwe ngokweSahluko 37C soMthetho WeSikhwama Sempesheni. ABasingethe baqoka ukusabalalisa kwezimali futhi abakhinyabezwa kuphela yikhethelo lakho.

Inzuzo Yomngcwabo

U-SAMRO unohlelo lwenzuzo yomngcwabo ukusiza wena nomndeni wakho oseduzane ngezindleko zomngcwabo. Xhumana ne-SAMRO ukuze uthole imininingwane.
